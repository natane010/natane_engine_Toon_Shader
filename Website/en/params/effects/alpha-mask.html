Alpha Mask

Partially control transparency using a texture

Alpha Mask uses the R (red) channel of a texture to partially control the transparency of a material. It enables complex transparency control such as making only part of glass transparent, creating see-through sections in clothing, or fading out with gradients.

Keyword: _ALPHA_MASK

Alpha Mask Settings

Alpha Mask _AlphaMask texture

A mask texture that partially controls the material's transparency using the R channel.

  • White (1.0) = Fully opaque
  • Gray (0.5) = Semi-transparent
  • Black (0.0) = Fully transparent

Default: "white" (fully opaque)

To use semi-transparency effects, set the rendering mode to "Transparent." Alpha mask does not function in Opaque mode.

Primary Uses

Glass Expression

Make only part of glass transparent to express frosted glass or patterned glass.

See-Through Effect

Make parts of clothing or veils semi-transparent to express see-through materials.

Fade Out

Use gradient textures to create fade-out effects that make edges transparent.

Complex Shape Transparency Control

Control the transparency of complex patterns like lace or mesh materials with a single mask texture.
