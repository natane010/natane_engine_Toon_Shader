Rim Light 2

A second rim light layer with different color and intensity

Overview

Rim Light 2 is a second rim light layer independent from Rim Light 1. By layering two rim light layers, you can achieve more complex and three-dimensional rim light expressions.

Controlled by the keyword _RIM_LIGHT_2. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Recommended Multi-Layer Rim Light Settings

Tips for effectively using two rim light layers:

  • Rim Light 1: Bright color, low power (inner contour)
  • Rim Light 2: Pale color, high power (outer contour)

By combining different blend modes and colors, you can create rainbow contours or gradient effects.

Parameters

Rim Light 2 Color _RimColor2 color

Sets the color of the second rim light. Setting a different color from Rim Light 1 creates a gradient effect.

Default: (0.5, 0.8, 1, 1) (light blue)

Using complementary colors with Rim Light 1 creates a dreamy impression.
Rim Light 2 Power _RimPower2 range

Controls the sharpness of the second rim light. Setting a different power value from Rim Light 1 produces different contour effects for inner and outer areas.

Default: 5

Range: 0.1 - 10

Rim Light 2 Intensity _RimIntensity2 range

Controls the brightness (intensity) of the second rim light.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 - 5

Rim Spread 2 (Glow) _RimSpread2 range

Bleeds and spreads Rim Light 2.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1

Mask

Rim Mask 2 _RimMask2 texture

A mask texture that controls the display area of Rim Light 2. Enabled by the keyword _RIM_MASK_2. Independent from Rim Light 1's mask.

Default: "white" (full display)

White = rim light visible, Black = rim light hidden. Since a separate mask from Rim Light 1 can be used, different rim light settings per part are possible.

Blend & Blur

Blend Mode _RimBlendMode2 enum

Selects the blend method for Rim Light 2.

  • Normal (0) - Standard additive blending
  • Soft (1) - Soft additive. Prevents over-brightening
  • Screen (2) - Screen blending. Natural brightness addition
  • Overlay (3) - Overlay blending. Enhances contrast

Default: Normal (0)

Blend _RimBlend2 range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the Rim Light 2 effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Blur _Rim2Blur range

Adjusts the blur amount of the Rim Light 2 effect.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1
