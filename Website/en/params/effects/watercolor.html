Watercolor Effect

Simulate watercolor painting with edge darkening, wet edges, granulation, and paper texture

Overview

The Watercolor effect simulates the look of watercolor painting by combining four elements: (1) edge darkening driven by ddx/ddy gradient magnitude, (2) wet edge effects that increase saturation at color boundaries, (3) texture-based pigment granulation, and (4) screen-space paper texture overlay. Together these create an authentic watercolor painting aesthetic.

Controlled by the keyword _WATERCOLOR. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

This is a texture-space effect that does not require GrabPass. Available in all variants including Lite.

Parameters

Edge Darkening _WCEdgeDarkening range

Controls the intensity of darkening at areas with strong color transitions. Driven by ddx/ddy gradient magnitude, this simulates how watercolor ink accumulates and darkens at edges where colors meet.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 - 2

Values of 0.3-0.7 give a natural result. Values above 1.0 produce strong edge effects.
Wet Edge _WCWetEdge range

Controls the intensity of local saturation increase at edges. This simulates the phenomenon in watercolor painting where pigment concentrates at the edges as the water dries, creating more vivid color boundaries. Applied using a smoothstep function based on edge strength.

Default: 0.3

Range: 0 - 1

Granulation _WCGranulation range

Controls the intensity of pigment granulation. The granulation effect is stronger in shadowed areas, simulating how pigment particles settle into the valleys of the paper texture in darker regions.

Default: 0.4

Range: 0 - 1

Granulation Texture _WCGranulationTex texture

A texture representing pigment particle patterns. The R channel is used, sampled in UV space. Tiling and offset are configurable.

Default: "gray"

Noise textures or paper fiber patterns with irregular bumps work best for this effect.
Paper Texture _WCPaperTex texture

A texture representing the watercolor paper surface. The R channel is used, sampled in screen space for stable display regardless of camera movement.

Default: "white"

Paper Intensity _WCPaperIntensity range

Controls how strongly the paper texture affects the result. The texture value is remapped from [0,1] to [-1,1] and multiplied into the color.

Default: 0.3

Range: 0 - 1

Paper Tiling _WCPaperTiling float

Screen-space tiling multiplier for the paper texture. Higher values produce finer paper grain.

Default: 1

Blend _WCBlend range

Controls the overall intensity of the watercolor effect. Multiplied with the mask texture value for the final blend amount.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Mask

Watercolor Mask _WCMask texture

A mask texture that controls the application area of the watercolor effect. The R channel is used. Tiling and offset are configurable.

Default: "white" (full application)

White = watercolor effect applied, Black = no effect. Useful for keeping skin clean while applying the watercolor style to clothing, for example.

Usage Tips

Basic Watercolor Setup

The default values (Edge Darkening 0.5, Wet Edge 0.3, Granulation 0.4) provide a well-balanced starting point. Using an actual scanned watercolor paper image as the paper texture greatly enhances realism.

Combining with Color Bleeding

Pair with Color Bleeding for a more authentic watercolor look. The Watercolor effect handles texture-space qualities (edge darkening, paper grain) while Color Bleeding handles screen-space color diffusion, creating complementary results.

Lightweight Design (No GrabPass Required)

Unlike other illustration-style effects (Kuwahara, Color Bleeding, etc.), the Watercolor effect operates using only ddx/ddy and texture sampling, requiring no GrabPass. This means it works in Lite variants and has relatively low performance cost.

Granulation Texture Selection

The granulation effect intensifies proportionally to the shading value (shadow darkness). This naturally reproduces the physical behavior of watercolor paint where pigment settles in darker washed areas.
