SSS (Subsurface Scattering)

Subsurface Scattering for skin-like translucency

Overview

SSS (Subsurface Scattering) is an effect that simulates light passing through objects. It is ideal for expressing materials where light penetrates and scatters beneath the surface, such as human skin, leaves, candles, and thin cloth.

Controlled by the keyword _SSS. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Parameters

SSS Color _SSSColor color

Sets the color of the subsurface scattered light. For skin, reddish tones produce a natural impression.

Default: (1, 0.5, 0.5, 1) (light red)

For skin, warm tones of (1, 0.5, 0.5) to (1, 0.7, 0.6) are recommended. For leaves, green tones like (0.5, 1, 0.3) work well.
SSS Intensity _SSSIntensity range

Controls the overall intensity of the subsurface scattering effect.

Default: 1.5

Range: 0 - 5

SSS Power _SSSPower range

Controls the concentration of scattered light. Higher values concentrate scattering to a narrow area, while lower values spread it over a wider area.

Default: 3

Range: 0.1 - 10

SSS Distortion _SSSDistortion range

Controls the directional distortion of scattered light. Sets how much the scattering is distorted along the normal direction, creating more natural scattering patterns.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 - 1

Thickness Map

Thickness Map _ThicknessMap texture

A texture that stores thickness information of the object. Enabled by the keyword _THICKNESS_MAP. Thinner areas allow more light to pass through.

Default: "white" (uniform thickness)

White = thin (strong SSS), Black = thick (weak SSS). Painting thin areas like ears, nose, and fingertips as white produces effective results.
Thickness Scale _ThicknessScale range

Scales the influence of the thickness map. Lower values reduce the variation in thickness.

Default: 0.2

Range: 0 - 1

Mask

SSS Mask _SSSMask texture

A mask texture that controls the application area of SSS. Enabled by the keyword _SSS_MASK.

Default: "white" (full application)

White = SSS applied, Black = SSS hidden. It is common to apply SSS only to skin areas while excluding clothing and metallic parts.

Blend & Blur

Blend Mode _SSSBlendMode enum

Selects the blend method for the SSS effect.

  • Normal (0) - Standard additive blending
  • Soft (1) - Soft additive. Prevents over-brightening
  • Screen (2) - Screen blending. Natural brightness addition
  • Overlay (3) - Overlay blending. Enhances contrast

Default: Normal (0)

Blend _SSSBlend range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the SSS effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Blur _SSSBlur range

Adjusts the blur amount of the SSS effect.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1
