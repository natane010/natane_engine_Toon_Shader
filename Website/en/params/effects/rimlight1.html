Rim Light 1

Add light to object edges to enhance depth

Overview

Rim Light is an effect that adds light to areas where the view direction and the object's normals are nearly perpendicular (edges). It enhances the three-dimensional appearance of characters and makes them stand out from the background.

Controlled by the keyword _RIM_LIGHT. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Parameters

Rim Light Color _RimColor color

Sets the color of the rim light. You can choose a color that matches the ambient light or the character's atmosphere.

Default: (1, 1, 1, 1) (white)

Orange tones are recommended for sunset scenes, and blue tones for underwater scenes.
Rim Light Power _RimPower range

Controls the sharpness of the rim light. Higher values concentrate the effect on thin edge areas, while lower values spread it over a wider area.

Default: 3

Range: 0.1 - 10

Values of 5-8 are recommended for delicate contour-line effects, and 1-2 for a soft aura-like effect.
Rim Light Intensity _RimIntensity range

Controls the brightness (intensity) of the rim light. Values exceeding 1.0 produce HDR-like glow effects.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 5

Rim Spread (Glow) _RimSpread range

Bleeds and spreads the rim light, adding a soft glowing appearance.

  • 0 - No spread (sharp rim)
  • 0.3-0.5 - Moderate spread (recommended)
  • 0.7-1.0 - Large spread (strong glow)

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1

The glow effect works best when combined with low power values for a dreamy, ethereal look.

Mask

Rim Mask _RimMask texture

A mask texture that controls the display area of the rim light. Enabled by the keyword _RIM_MASK.

Default: "white" (full display)

White = rim light visible, Black = rim light hidden. Useful for concentrating rim light on the face or excluding parts of the clothing.

Blend & Blur

Blend Mode _RimBlendMode enum

Selects the blend method for the rim light effect.

  • Normal (0) - Standard additive blending
  • Soft (1) - Soft additive. Prevents over-brightening
  • Screen (2) - Screen blending. Natural brightness addition
  • Overlay (3) - Overlay blending. Enhances contrast

Default: Normal (0)

Blend _RimBlend range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the rim light effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Blur _RimBlur range

Adjusts the blur amount of the rim light effect.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1
