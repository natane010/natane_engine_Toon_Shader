AudioLink

Music-reactive effects with VRChat AudioLink

In VRChat AudioLink-enabled worlds, shader effects change in real time in sync with the beat and rhythm of music. Emission, rim light, hue shift, dissolve, and outline effects can each be individually linked to music. An ideal effect for clubs and live events.

Keyword: _AUDIOLINK

About Frequency Bands

AudioLink Frequency Bands

You can select the frequency band used by each effect.

  • Bass (0) - Bass range. Responds to kick drums and bass lines.
  • Low Mid (1) - Low-mid range. Responds to snare drums and guitar lows.
  • High Mid (2) - High-mid range. Responds to vocals and melodies.
  • Treble (3) - Treble range. Responds to hi-hats and cymbals.

Emission Link

Frequency Band _AudioLinkEmissionBand enum

Selects the frequency band that emission (glow) responds to. Enabled by the keyword _AUDIOLINK_EMISSION.

Default: 0 (Bass)

Emission Intensity _AudioLinkEmissionIntensity range

Sets the glow intensity of music-linked emission.

Default: 1 (Range: 0 - 5)

Blend Settings

Blend Mode _AudioLinkBlendMode enum

Selects the compositing method for the overall AudioLink effect.

  • Normal (0) - Normal blending
  • Soft (1) - Soft light
  • Screen (2) - Screen
  • Overlay (3) - Overlay

Default: 0 (Normal)

Blend _AudioLinkBlend range

Sets the overall application amount of the AudioLink effect.

Default: 1 (Range: 0 - 1)
