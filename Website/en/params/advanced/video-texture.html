Video Texture

Blend control for video textures

Overview

The video texture feature displays video content on materials using a RenderTexture. Used for displaying video content on VRChat screens, monitor representations, in-game TV screens, and similar scenarios.

Controlled by the keyword _VIDEO_TEXTURE.

Properties

Enable Video Texture _VideoTexture toggle

Toggles the video texture feature on/off.

Default: OFF

Video Render Texture _VideoTex texture

The RenderTexture for the video content. Uses RenderTextures output from VRChat's AVPro video player or Unity's Video Player.

Default: "black"

In VRChat, assigning the AVPro Video Player's RenderTexture allows displaying in-world video content.
Video Emission Intensity _VideoEmission range

The emission intensity of the video texture. Controls the glowing screen effect. Can be adjusted so the video is visible even in dark environments.

Default: 1.0

Increasing the emission value makes the screen appear brightly lit even in dark areas.
Video Blend _VideoBlend range

The application intensity of the video texture. 0 for hidden, 1 for fully visible.

Default: 1.0

Video Blend Mode _VideoBlendMode enum

The compositing method for the video texture. Choose Replace to completely replace the base texture, or other modes to blend.

Default: 0 (Replace)

Usage Tips

  • Ideal for VRChat screens and monitor representations
  • Adjust emission intensity to match the screen appearance to the environment brightness
  • When no video is playing, the base texture is displayed
  • The RenderTexture resolution directly affects video quality
