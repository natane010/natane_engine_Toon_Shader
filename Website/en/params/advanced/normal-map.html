Normal Map

Simulate surface bumps with normal maps

Overview

Normal maps are a technique that adds the appearance of surface bumps without actually increasing polygon count. By storing normal direction information in the RGB channels of a texture and modifying surface orientation during lighting calculations, they realistically represent carved details, wrinkles, fabric textures, and more.

Controlled by the keyword _NORMALMAP; when disabled, shader instructions are zero-cost.

Properties

Use Normal Map _UseNormalMap toggle

Toggles the normal map feature on/off. When disabled, the shader keyword _NORMALMAP is turned off, resulting in zero processing cost.

Default: OFF

Normal Map _BumpMap texture

A texture containing normal direction information. Use textures imported with Unity's "Normal map" type. Supports tangent-space normal maps.

Default: "bump" (flat normal)

Setting "Texture Type" to "Normal map" in the texture import settings automatically applies the appropriate compression format.
Normal Scale _BumpScale range

Controls the intensity of the normal map. Higher values increase the bump effect; setting to 0 disables the effect.

Default: 1.0

For skin, 0.3-0.7 is recommended; for stone or walls, 1.0 or higher works well.

Usage Tips

  • In toon shading, normal maps affect the shadow boundary line
  • Combining with specular and rim lighting enables more three-dimensional expression
  • For VRChat, texture sizes of 1024x1024 to 2048x2048 are recommended
  • Since it is keyword-based, turning it OFF when not needed improves performance
