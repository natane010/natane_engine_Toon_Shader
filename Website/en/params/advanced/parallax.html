Parallax

Pseudo-depth with parallax mapping

Overview

Parallax (parallax mapping) is a technique that creates the illusion of depth on a surface using a height map. By offsetting texture coordinates based on the camera's viewing angle, it adds a sense of three-dimensional depth to cobblestones, brick walls, tiles, and similar surfaces without deforming the actual geometry.

Controlled by the keyword _PARALLAX.

Properties

Enable Parallax Mapping _Parallax toggle

Toggles the parallax mapping feature on/off. Branches at compile time using the keyword _PARALLAX.

Default: OFF

Height Map _ParallaxMap texture

A grayscale texture containing surface height information. White areas are raised, and black areas are lowered.

Default: "black"

Height maps are most effective when used together with normal maps.
Parallax Scale _ParallaxScale range

Controls the strength of the parallax effect. Increasing the value provides a deeper sense of depth, but values too high may cause unnatural artifacts.

Default: 0.02

Adjusting within the 0.01-0.05 range is recommended.
Minimum Samples _ParallaxMinSamples range

The number of ray marching samples when viewing from the front. Fewer samples reduce processing load but decrease quality.

Default: 4

Maximum Samples _ParallaxMaxSamples range

The maximum number of ray marching samples when viewing at steep angles. More samples are needed at steep angles, so this value balances quality and performance.

Default: 16

For VRChat Quest, keeping the maximum sample count to 8-12 can reduce performance impact.

Usage Tips

  • Best suited for hard surfaces like stone and walls
  • Combining normal maps with height maps produces the most realistic results
  • Keep the scale value modest to prevent visual artifacts
  • Increasing sample count improves quality but also increases GPU load
