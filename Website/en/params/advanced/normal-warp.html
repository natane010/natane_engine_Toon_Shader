Normal Warp

Procedural normal deformation for shading control

Overview

Normal Warp procedurally deforms world-space normals to adjust how toon shading applies to a surface. It combines two operations: blending toward a spherical normal (Roundness) and flattening the Y component (Flatten Y) to reshape the mesh normals.

Controlled by the keyword _NORMAL_WARP. When disabled, no shader instructions are generated. Implemented as a local keyword (shader_feature_local).

Parameters

Enable Normal Warping _NormalWarp toggle

Toggles the Normal Warp feature on/off. Branches at compile time using the keyword _NORMAL_WARP.

Default: OFF

Normal Flatten Y _NormalFlattenY range

Controls how much the Y component (vertical direction) of the world-space normal is suppressed. Higher values flatten the Y component, reducing vertical shading variations. Useful for making character face shading more uniform.

Default: 0

Range: 0 – 1

On face materials, setting 0.3–0.7 reduces shadow artifacts under the nose.
Normal Roundness (Spherical Blend) _NormalRoundness range

Controls the blend strength toward a spherical normal derived from the object-space position. The spherical normal is generated by normalizing the direction vector from the object center. At 0 the original normal is kept; at 1 the normal is fully replaced by the spherical normal. This makes shading appear more uniform and rounded even on complex meshes.

Default: 0

Range: 0 – 1

On hair meshes, setting 0.2–0.5 smooths out shading seams.

Usage Tips

  • Most effective for controlling character face shading. Y flattening reduces unnatural shadows on the nose and cheeks
  • Spherical normal blending is useful for hair and clothing where polygon seams negatively affect shading
  • Both parameters can be combined. A good approach is to first set the spherical base, then fine-tune with Y flattening
  • Normals are deformed in the fragment shader before lighting calculations, so it works alongside normal maps
  • This feature only affects lighting normals; vertex positions and outlines are not changed
