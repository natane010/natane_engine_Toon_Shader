Surface Finish

Control surface quality through glossiness and matte effects

Glossiness _Glossiness range

Adjusts the intensity of all reflection-based effects such as specular, reflection, and rim light at once. 0 for no gloss (fully matte), 0.5 for moderate gloss, 1.0 for maximum gloss (default).

Affected effects: Specular highlight, Rim Light (1 & 2), Environment Rim, MatCap, Cubemap Reflection, Light Volume Specular. Does not affect Emission.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 1

Set 0.3-0.5 for materials that don't need gloss like cloth or skin, and 0.8-1.0 for metal or leather.
Matte Effect (Additional Gloss Reduction) _MatteEffect range

An additional parameter to further reduce gloss on top of the glossiness setting. 0 for glossiness-only control, 0.5 to reduce glossiness by 50%, 1.0 for fully matte (zero gloss).

Combined with glossiness for finer control. Example: Glossiness 0.8 x Matte Effect 0.3 = effective 56% gloss.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Convenient when you want to add a matte feel without lowering the glossiness. Can be combined with a texture mask to apply matte effects partially.
