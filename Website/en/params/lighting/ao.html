Ambient Occlusion

Enhance contact shadows for realistic shading

Ambient Occlusion (AO) darkens areas where ambient light has difficulty reaching, such as crevices and cavities, creating a more three-dimensional and soft impression. Ideal for expressing natural shadows in clothing folds, overlapping hair, inner ears, and similar areas.

AO Map _AOMap texture

The ambient occlusion map texture. White = no shadow (bright), Black = full shadow (dark). Keyword: _USE_AO

Default: "white" (enabled via toggle)

Ideal for shadow expression in areas like armpits and necklines. AO maps can be baked from 3D modeling software or painted manually.
AO Intensity _AOIntensity range

Controls the strength of the AO effect. 0 for no effect, 1 for maximum effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 1

Recommended intensity: 0.5-0.8. Too strong can make shadows look unnatural.
Blend _AOBlend range

The blend intensity of the AO effect. Controls how much the effect is mixed into the base color.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 1

Blend Mode _AOBlendMode enum

Select the blending method for AO.

  • Normal (0): Standard blending
  • Soft (1): Soft blending
  • Screen (2): Screen
  • Overlay (3): Overlay

Default: Normal (0)

Blur _AOBlur range

The blur amount of the AO effect. 0 for sharp, 1 for maximum blur.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Adding blur creates softer, more natural AO boundaries. Also effective when texture resolution is low.
