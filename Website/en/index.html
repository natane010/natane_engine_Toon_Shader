What's New in v1.6.0

🙂

Face Ortho Projection

Blends perspective toward orthographic projection around a pivot, keeping face proportions ideal at any camera distance, FOV, or camera type. Separate VR strength, mask support, and outline tracking.
🪞

Mirror/Camera Alt Texture

Swaps the base look to an alternate texture/color only in VRChat mirrors or the camera. Build "a different self in the mirror" or "patterns that only appear in photos".
👻

Ghost Variant

A depth prepass + ZTest Equal translucent ghost that structurally avoids double-blend where body parts overlap and backface bleed-through. Fresnel center fade plus an HDR rim glow.
🎆

Particles / GPU Particles / Fake Fluid

A lightweight toon particle variant, plus stateless vertex-animated GPU particles (avatar-safe, AudioLink-ready) and a math-based in-container liquid shader.
👁️

Eye Setup Tool

An editor window for non-destructive eye material separation (triangle extraction into a new submesh) and UV relayout (overlap/side-by-side). The dedicated "Natane/Eye" shader works out of the box afterwards.
🚀

Unity 6 URP Support

A conditionally-included URP SubShader on the main shader, with SRP Batcher CBUFFERs and GPU Resident Drawer (DOTS instancing) support, covering the core features with the same properties as BiRP.

🛡️

VRCFallback Support

VRCFallback tags added to all variants, so safety-blocked avatars fall back to a sane toon shader.

🕳️

Stencil Presets

One-click Writer / Reader (Equal) / Reader (NotEqual) presets for building peepholes, hidden patterns, and vanishing-body effects with ease.
📦

Automatic Build Optimization

Automatically strips shader variants with unused keywords, hooks VRChat SDK uploads (keyword sync + Lite conversion suggestions), and emits a post-build optimization report.
💡

Lighting Quality Fixes

Fixed a light-intensity clamp bug that dimmed white light by about 42%, and added an fwidth-based minimum AA band on toon boundaries to suppress shimmer in VR.
