MatCap 1

Realistic environment reflections using Material Capture textures

Overview

MatCap (Material Capture) is an effect that uses spherically captured material environment maps to render realistic reflections and textures. By sampling textures based on normal directions, it achieves high-quality environment reflections with low computational cost.

Controlled by the keyword _MATCAP. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Parameters

MatCap Texture _MatCapTex texture

Sets the MatCap texture (spherical reflection map). Uses a square texture that captures the appearance of a material applied to a sphere.

Default: "black" (black texture)

The MatCap texture must be a spherical reflection map. Searching for "MatCap texture" online will yield many free resources.
MatCap Intensity _MatCapIntensity range

Controls the intensity of the MatCap effect. Values exceeding 1.0 produce HDR-like strong reflection effects.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 2

MatCap Blend Mode _MatCapBlendMode enum

Selects the blend method for the MatCap effect.

  • Add (0) - Additive blending. Adds reflection light to brighten
  • Multiply (1) - Multiply blending. Darker areas become even darker
  • Replace (2) - Replace. Completely overwrites with the MatCap texture

Default: Add (0)

Add is suited for metallic reflections, Multiply for dirt and scratch expressions, and Replace for special stylized looks.

Mask

MatCap Mask _MatCapMask texture

A mask texture that controls the application area of MatCap. Enabled by the keyword _MATCAP_MASK.

Default: "white" (full application)

White = MatCap applied, Black = MatCap hidden. Useful for applying MatCap only to metallic parts.

Blend & Blur

Blend _MatCapBlend range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the MatCap effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Blur _MatCapBlur range

Adjusts the blur amount of the MatCap effect. Adding blur creates soft reflection expressions.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1
