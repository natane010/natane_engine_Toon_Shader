Screen Tone Effect

Halftone/screentone overlay pattern for manga-style rendering

Overview

The Screen Tone effect adds manga/comic-style halftone dot patterns over the surface. It is useful for stylized NPR (Non-Photorealistic Rendering), reproducing the characteristic screentone shading found in printed manga and pop art illustrations.

Controlled by the keyword _SCREEN_TONE. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Parameters

Screen Tone Color _ScreenToneColor color

Sets the color of the halftone dots.

Default: (0, 0, 0, 1) (black)

Black produces classic manga-style screentone. Try colored dots for pop art or retro comic effects.
Screen Tone Scale _ScreenToneScale range

Controls the density and scale of the halftone dot pattern. Lower values produce larger dots, higher values produce finer dots.

Default: 10

Range: 1 - 200

Use low Scale (5-10) for large dots (pop art style), high Scale (50-100) for fine print-style halftone. Values around 20-30 give a classic manga feel.
Screen Tone Threshold _ScreenToneThreshold range

Sets the brightness threshold for dot visibility. Areas darker than this threshold will display the screentone dots.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 - 1

Lower values restrict dots to darker shadow areas only. Higher values extend dots into brighter areas. Combine with shadow areas for manga-style shading.

Mask

Screen Tone Mask _ScreenToneMask texture

A mask texture that controls the display area of the screen tone effect.

Default: "white" (full display)

White = screentone visible, Black = screentone hidden. Useful for applying screentone only to clothing or shadow areas while keeping skin clean.

Blend & Blur

Blend Mode _ScreenToneBlendMode enum

Selects the blend method for the screen tone effect.

  • Normal (0) - Standard blending
  • Soft (1) - Soft blending. Prevents harsh transitions
  • Screen (2) - Screen blending. Natural brightness addition
  • Overlay (3) - Overlay blending. Enhances contrast

Default: Normal (0)

Blend _ScreenToneBlend range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the screen tone effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Blur _ScreenToneBlur range

Adjusts the blur amount of the screen tone effect. Softens dot edges for a smoother appearance.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1
