Water Drop Effect

Animated water droplets trickling down the surface

Overview

The Water Drop Effect is an animation effect that adds water droplets dripping down a surface. It is suitable for expressing rain-soaked clothing or glass surfaces.

Controlled by the keyword _WATER_DRIP. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Parameters

Drop Color _DripColor color

Sets the color of the water drops. Translucent colors produce a natural finish.

Default: (0.7, 0.85, 1.0, 1) (pale blue-white)

For realistic water drops, use (0.7, 0.85, 1.0) pale blue-white. For blood effects, red tones like (0.8, 0.1, 0.1) can also be used.
Drop Speed _DripSpeed range

Controls the falling speed of the drops. Higher values make them fall faster.

Default: 1

Range: 0.1 - 5

0.3-0.5 is recommended for gentle rain, and 2-3 for heavy rain.
Drop Density _DripDensity range

Controls the frequency of drop appearance. Higher values display more drops.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 - 1

0 = sparse, 1 = dense. Low density (0.1-0.3) suits light sweat or condensation, while high density (0.7-1.0) works for heavy rain.
Drop Size _DripSize range

Controls the size of individual drops.

Default: 0.15

Range: 0.01 - 0.5

Drop Trail Length _DripTrailLength range

Controls the length of the water trail left by drops. Higher values produce longer trails.

Default: 1.0

Range: 0 - 3

Setting to 0 removes the trail, displaying only round water drops. Long trails are ideal for rain flowing down window glass.
Drop Intensity _DripIntensity range

Controls the brightness (intensity) of the effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 2

Drop Sharpness _DripSharpness range

Controls the edge clarity of the drops. Higher values produce sharper outlines, while lower values create blurred outlines.

Default: 2

Range: 0.5 - 5

Mask

Drop Mask _DripMask texture

A mask texture that controls the display area of the drops. Enabled by the keyword _DRIP_MASK. Uses the R (red) channel to control the drop display area.

Default: "white" (full display)

White (1.0) = fully visible, Black (0.0) = hidden, Gray = partially visible. Use a mask texture to restrict the area where drops flow.

Blend & Blur

Blend Mode _DripBlendMode enum

Selects the blend method for the water drop effect.

  • Normal (0) - Standard additive blending
  • Soft (1) - Soft additive. Prevents over-brightening
  • Screen (2) - Screen blending. Natural brightness addition
  • Overlay (3) - Overlay blending. Enhances contrast

Default: Normal (0)

Blend _DripBlend range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the water drop effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Blur _DripBlur range

Adjusts the blur amount of the water drop effect.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1

Usage Tips

Expressing Rain-Soaked Clothing or Glass Surfaces

  • Speed: Drop falling speed
  • Density: Drop appearance frequency (0=sparse, 1=dense)
  • Size: Drop size
  • Trail Length: Length of water trails left by drops
  • Intensity: Effect brightness
  • Sharpness: Drop edge clarity

By using mask textures to restrict the flow area, you can create natural expressions such as rain dripping from shoulders or condensation forming on only part of a window.
