Hair Specular

Kajiya-Kay dual-lobe anisotropic hair highlight

Overview

Hair Specular implements the Kajiya-Kay hair shading model with two specular lobes for realistic anime-style hair highlights. The primary lobe provides sharp highlights, while the secondary lobe adds softer fill. This creates the characteristic dual-band hair shine seen in anime and CG rendering.

Controlled by the keyword _HAIR_SPECULAR. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Primary Lobe

Primary Highlight Color _HairSpecColor1 color

Sets the color of the primary (sharp) hair highlight.

Default: (1, 1, 1, 1) (white)

White or light versions of the hair color produce the most natural results. Colored highlights can create fantasy or stylized effects.
Primary Tangent Shift _HairSpecShift1 range

Shifts the primary highlight along the hair tangent direction. Positive values move the highlight toward the hair tips, negative values toward the roots.

Default: 0.1

Range: -1 - 1

Slightly positive values (0.05-0.2) produce the most natural-looking highlight placement for anime hair.
Primary Sharpness _HairSpecWidth1 range

Controls the sharpness of the primary highlight. Higher values produce a tighter, sharper highlight band.

Default: 64

Range: 1 - 256

For anime hair, use high values (96-128) for a crisp highlight band. Lower values (16-32) give a softer, more realistic look.

Secondary Lobe

Secondary Highlight Color _HairSpecColor2 color

Sets the color of the secondary (soft fill) hair highlight. Typically dimmer than the primary.

Default: (0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 1) (grey)

Secondary Tangent Shift _HairSpecShift2 range

Shifts the secondary highlight along the hair tangent direction. Usually set opposite to the primary shift for realistic dual-band appearance.

Default: -0.1

Range: -1 - 1

Secondary Sharpness _HairSpecWidth2 range

Controls the sharpness of the secondary highlight. Typically set lower than the primary for a softer fill effect.

Default: 32

Range: 1 - 256

General Settings

Overall Intensity _HairSpecIntensity range

Controls the overall intensity of both hair specular lobes.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 2

Mask & Shift Texture

Hair Specular Mask _HairSpecMask texture

A mask texture that controls the display area of the hair specular effect.

Default: "white" (full display)

White = highlight visible, Black = highlight hidden. Use to restrict highlights to hair-only areas and prevent them from appearing on skin or accessories.
Shift Texture _HairSpecShiftTex texture

A texture that adds per-pixel variation to the tangent shift. Creates natural irregularity along hair strands, preventing a perfectly uniform highlight band.

Default: "grey" (neutral, no shift variation)

Use a noise or gradient texture to break up the highlight band. The Shift Texture adds natural variation along hair strands for a more convincing look.

Blend & Distance Fade

Blend Mode _HairSpecBlendMode enum

Selects the blend method for the hair specular effect.

  • Normal (0) - Standard additive blending
  • Soft (1) - Soft additive. Prevents over-brightening
  • Screen (2) - Screen blending. Natural brightness addition
  • Overlay (3) - Overlay blending. Enhances contrast

Default: Normal (0)

Blend _HairSpecBlend range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the hair specular effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Distance Fade _HairSpecDistFade range

Fades the hair specular effect based on camera distance. Useful for LOD optimization.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1
