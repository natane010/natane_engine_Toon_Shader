Line Boil

Lines wobble in frame steps like hand-drawn animation. Outlines, hatching, and watercolor grain snap to a new shape every few frames for the warmth of pencil or crayon art. (v1.6.0)

PREVIEW Line Boil

Overview

This reproduces the "line boil" of hand-drawn animation. Instead of wobbling smoothly, time is quantized to a fixed number of frames and the noise seed is switched on those beats. The drawn line therefore gets replaced frame by frame, giving the classic feel of animating on 2s or 3s.

It toggles with the keyword _LINE_BOIL and has zero effect when off (zero cost). It applies as position/width jitter on the outline pass, and as UV jitter on hatching, screen tone, and watercolor grain in the fragment stage.

What you can make

  • Pencil-drawn outlines and animation-key-frame-style line wobble
  • Picture-book, crayon, and charcoal textures
  • Manga hatching that subtly drifts
  • Watercolor paper grain that changes every frame
  • Lines that thrash to the beat when combined with AudioLink (via the FX Modulator)

Key parameters

Boil FPS _LineBoilFPS range (1–24)

How many times per second the jitter refreshes. Lower is choppier and more old-school; higher trembles more finely.

Default: 8

Around 12 for clean anime, 8 for pencil, and 4 for a stop-motion feel.
Hold Frames _LineBoilHoldFrames range (1–8)

How many frames each shape is held. 2 gives "animating on 2s", 3 gives "on 3s".

Default: 1

Set 2–3 for the deliberate pacing of Japanese animation.
Position Jitter _LineBoilPositionJitter range (0–5)

How much the outline vertices wobble (×0.001 object space). Higher makes the line thrash more boldly.

Default: 1

Larger for rough sketches, smaller for clean line art.
Width Jitter _LineBoilWidthJitter range (0–1)

How much the outline width varies per frame. Adds the pressure variation of a hand-drawn line.

Default: 0.2

UV Jitter _LineBoilUVJitter range (0–0.05)

How much the UVs of hatching, screen tone, and watercolor drift. Paper grain and halftone dots shift each frame.

Default: 0.005

Usage Tips

  • Choose the targets: _LineBoilAffectOutline / AffectHatching / AffectWatercolor toggle each target independently. Turn the others off to wobble only the line.
  • Cohesive hand-drawn look: boil Watercolor, Screen Tone, Outline, and Color Quantize at the same low FPS so line, shadow, and paper grain move as one.
  • Mask: use the R channel of _LineBoilMaskTex to limit where the boil applies.
  • Vertex positions themselves never change, so there is no FORWARD_ADD desync (misalignment under a second light). It also coexists with fwidth-based toon AA.
