MatCap 2/3

Additional MatCap layers for complex reflection effects

Overview

MatCap 2 and MatCap 3 are independent MatCap layers that can be stacked on top of MatCap 1. By layering multiple MatCap textures with different blend modes, you can achieve more complex and expressive environment reflections.

Each layer is individually controlled by the keywords _MATCAP_2 and _MATCAP_3. No shader instructions are generated when disabled.

Multi-MatCap Usage Examples

  • MatCap 1: Main environment reflection (Add mode)
  • MatCap 2: Metallic highlights (Add mode, high intensity)
  • MatCap 3: Dirt and scratch expression (Multiply mode)

By using different masks, you can simultaneously express different textures such as metal, cloth, and leather within a single material.

MatCap 2

MatCap 2 Texture _MatCapTex2 texture

Sets the second MatCap texture (spherical reflection map).

Default: "black" (black texture)

MatCap 2 Intensity _MatCapIntensity2 range

Controls the intensity of the MatCap 2 effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 2

MatCap 2 Blend Mode _MatCapBlendMode2 enum

Selects the blend method for MatCap 2.

  • Add (0) - Additive blending
  • Multiply (1) - Multiply blending
  • Replace (2) - Replace

Default: Add (0)

MatCap 2 Mask

MatCap 2 Mask _MatCapMask2 texture

A mask texture that controls the application area of MatCap 2. Enabled by the keyword _MATCAP_MASK_2.

Default: "white" (full application)

White = MatCap applied, Black = not applied. Independent from MatCap 1's mask, allowing different MatCap applications to different parts.

MatCap 2 Blend & Blur

Blend _MatCapBlend2 range

Controls the intensity (opacity) of the MatCap 2 effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 - 1

Blur _MatCap2Blur range

Adjusts the blur amount of the MatCap 2 effect.

Default: 0

Range: 0 - 1

Performance Notes

Shader load increases as more MatCap layers are added. For VRChat Quest environments, it is recommended to limit MatCap to 1 layer.

  • MatCap 1 only: Minimal performance impact
  • MatCap 1+2: Moderate load increase
  • MatCap 1+2+3: Significant load increase, recommended for PC only
