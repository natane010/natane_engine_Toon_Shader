Mirror/Camera Alt Texture

Swap the look only when seen in a mirror or camera (v1.6.0)

Overview

Mirror/Camera Alt Texture swaps the base color to an alternate texture and color only when the material is rendered in a VRChat mirror or by the VRChat camera. It enables mysterious effects such as "plain clothes to the eye, but a pattern appears in the mirror" or "a message that only shows up in photos".

Shading (toon shadows, rim light, etc.) still applies to the swapped look. To fully hide or show the material in mirrors, use Mirror Control instead.

Controlled by the keyword _MIRROR_TEXTURE.

Parameters

Alt Texture _MirrorAltTex texture

The alternate texture shown in mirrors/cameras. Sampled with the same UVs as the main texture.

Default: white

Start from the main texture and paint extra patterns or messages onto it for an easy "different self in the mirror".
Alt Color _MirrorAltColor color

Color multiplied onto the alt texture. Works without a texture for simple recolor effects.

Default: (1, 1, 1, 1)

Leaving the texture empty and changing only this color gives a quick "recolored mirror self".
Blend _MirrorTexBlend range

Blend amount from the normal look to the alt look. 0 = normal appearance, 1 = fully swapped.

Default: 1.0

Around 0.5 creates a semi-transparent "double self" effect.
Apply to Mirror _MirrorTexApplyMirror toggle

Whether the swap applies to VRChat mirror reflections.

Default: ON

Turn this OFF and enable only "Apply to Camera" for photo-only effects.
Apply to Camera _MirrorTexApplyCamera toggle

Whether the swap applies to the VRChat camera (handheld / desktop / screenshots).

Default: OFF

Enable for messages or watermarks that only appear in photos.

Usage Tips

  • A different self in the mirror: enable Apply to Mirror and set the transformed look as the alt texture — a classic horror/fantasy trick.
  • Photo-only watermark: enable Apply to Camera so signatures or patterns appear only in screenshots.
  • Double self: set Blend to 0.5 for a ghostly overlay of two appearances.
  • Uses VRChat-specific global variables; the feature has no effect outside VRChat.
  • Related: Mirror Control (controls visibility itself in mirrors)
