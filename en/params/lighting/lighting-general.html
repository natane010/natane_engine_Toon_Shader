General Lighting

Control light source intensity, indirect light, and light color influence

Global Light Control

Light Intensity (Global) _LightIntensity range

Controls the overall lighting intensity. 0 for no light, 1 for standard, 2 for brighter.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 2

Indirect Light Intensity _IndirectLightIntensity range

Controls the intensity of indirect illumination from ambient light and light probes.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 2

GI Intensity (Environment Reflection) _GIIntensity range

Controls the influence of environment reflection (Light Probes/GI). 0 to completely disable environment reflection (unaffected by ambient light), 0.5 to reduce environment reflection by 50%, 1 to apply environment reflection normally.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Use this to stabilize appearance in VRChat worlds that are too dark or have overly strong lighting.
Light Color Influence _LightColorInfluence range

Controls how much the light's color affects the material. 0 to ignore light color (treat as white light), 0.5 for moderate (recommended), 1 to fully reflect light color.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 1

Lower this value to stabilize the material's color in worlds with strongly colored lights.

Shadow Settings

Shadow Receive _ShadowReceive range

Controls how much shadows from other objects affect this material. 1 for full shadow, 0 for no shadow.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 1

Shadow Max Darkness _ShadowMaxDarkness range

Controls the minimum brightness of shadows. 0 for fully dark, 1 for no darkening. Prevents shadows from becoming completely black.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

In worlds where shadows become extremely dark, setting to 0.2-0.4 improves appearance.
Shadow Receive Mask _ShadowReceiveMask texture

A mask texture for partial control of shadow receiving. White = no shadow received (kept bright, shading also disabled), Black = full shadow received (normal shadow and shading), Gray = partial shadow. Keyword: _SHADOW_RECEIVE_MASK

Default: "white" (enabled via toggle)

If the face gets dark from hair shadows, use a mask with the face area painted white to prevent shadows from falling on the face. A commonly used technique for VRChat avatars. Also controls Light Volume indirect light (rim effects).

Light Influence Range

Light Min Influence _LightMinInfluence range

Sets the minimum lighting value. Prevents areas from becoming too dark.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Light Max Influence _LightMaxInfluence range

Sets the maximum lighting value. Prevents overexposure.

Default: 2

Range: 1 to 5

Light Softness

Light Blending (Softness) _LightBlend range

Blends lighting transitions with surroundings for a smoother result. 0 for sharp transitions (default), 0.3-0.5 for moderate softness (recommended), 0.7-1.0 for very soft transitions.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Adjust this when light intensity changes too abruptly.
Highlight Softness _HighlightSoftness range

Blends bright areas (highlights) with their surroundings. 0 for sharp highlights (default), 0.3-0.5 for moderate softness (recommended), 0.7-1.0 for very soft highlights.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Adjust this when highlights are too strong or when you want a softer impression. Setting all three -- shadow blending, light blending, and highlight softness -- above 0.5 may cause shading to become very blurry. It is recommended to choose 1-2 main parameters and keep the others below 0.3.

Additional Light

Additional Light Intensity _AdditionalLightIntensity range

Controls the intensity of additional lights (ForwardAdd pass). Lower values prevent overbright when using multiple lights. 0 for no additional lights, 1 for full intensity.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 to 1

Lower values provide stability in VRChat club worlds and other environments with many lights.
