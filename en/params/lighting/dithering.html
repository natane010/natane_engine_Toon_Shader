Dithering

Add dither patterns to shading

Dithering (halftone) adds a dot pattern to shadow boundaries, creating a softer and more artistic impression. Ideal for print-style or manga-style soft shadow expression (screen tone).

Pattern Size _DitheringScale range

Controls the size of the dither pattern. Smaller values for fine patterns, larger values for coarse patterns.

Default: 10

Range: 1 to 100

Recommended: 5-20. Use 10-20 for manga-style screen tone, 5-10 for subtle dithering.
Border Softness _DitheringStrength range

Controls the strength of the dithering effect (border blur amount). 0 for no effect, 1 for maximum effect.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 to 1

Recommended: 0.3-0.7. Too low and it's barely visible; too high and the pattern becomes overly prominent.
Blend _DitheringBlend range

The blend intensity of the dithering effect.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 1

Blur _DitheringBlur range

The blur amount of the dithering effect. 0 for sharp patterns, 1 for soft patterns.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Adding blur softens the pattern edges for a more natural finish.
