Soft Lighting

Soft and natural lighting adjustments

When soft lighting mode is enabled, all lighting and shading become globally softer. Shadow boundaries, light intensity, highlights, and more are all softened at once, making it ideal for illustration-style or watercolor-style soft expressions.

Soft Lighting Mode _SoftLightingMode toggle

Toggle to enable soft lighting mode. Globally softens all lighting and shading. Keyword: _SOFT_LIGHTING_MODE

Default: OFF

Soft Lighting Intensity _SoftLightingIntensity range

The intensity of soft lighting. Higher values make the overall lighting softer.

Default: 0.5

Range: 0 to 1

Recommended: 0.3-0.5 for moderate softness, 0.5-0.7 for quite soft, 0.7-1.0 for very soft. Easier to control overall softness than adjusting individual parameters.
