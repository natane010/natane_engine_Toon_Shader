Final Color Blend

Blending to prevent highlight blowout and shadow crushing

Highlight Blending

Highlight Blending (Blowout Prevention) _FinalHighlightBlend range

Naturally blends overly bright areas (highlights). Uses smooth shoulder tone mapping to naturally compress bright areas above 60% luminance, preventing blowout while preserving color vibrancy.

Processing flow: (1) Tone mapping compression → (2) Highlight blending (30% desaturation) → (3) Gentle clamp (allows up to 1.05)

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Recommended: 0.3-0.5 for normal use, 0.7-0.9 for strong effect, 1.0 for maximum.
Highlight Threshold _HighlightThreshold range

Additional highlight blending is applied to areas brighter than this value. Lower values apply blending to a wider range.

Default: 0.75

Range: 0 to 1

Recommended: 0.75. Set to 0.6 for a stronger effect, or 0.5 for maximum effect.

Shadow Blending

Shadow Blending (Crush Prevention) _FinalShadowBlend range

Blends overly dark areas (shadows) with their surroundings. Prevents shadow crushing and preserves detail. Softens shadow boundaries and slightly lifts shadows for a natural appearance.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Useful when detail is lost in dark worlds.
Shadow Threshold _ShadowThreshold range

Shadow blending is applied to areas darker than this value. Higher values apply the effect to a wider range.

Default: 0.25

Range: 0 to 1

Recommended: 0.25. Increase to 0.3-0.4 if shadows are very deep.
