Color Preservation & Enhancement

Adjust saturation and brightness while preserving texture colors

Texture Color Preservation _AlbedoPreservation range

Preserves the original texture color while applying lighting brightness effects. Instead of "becoming white" from lighting, the result "becomes brighter while keeping its original color."

The improved version maintains the hue while solving the problem of dark colors like black turning white under light. Hue and saturation are preserved while only adjusting brightness, and specular and rim light are naturally expressed according to the color.

Default: 0

Range: 0 to 1

Recommended: 0.7 to 0.9 (strong color preservation, most natural colors). Prevents black clothing from turning gray/white under light, and red clothing from turning pink/white. Setting too high reduces the influence of light color, so if you want blue light to illuminate objects blue, set it around 0.5.
Saturation _Saturation range

Adjusts the saturation (color vividness) of the final color. 0 for monochrome (grayscale), 1 for default (original saturation), 1.5 to 2.0 for vivid colors.

Default: 1

Range: 0 to 2

If texture color preservation is 0.7 or higher and saturation is 1.3 or higher, colors may become overly vivid. Recommended: if color preservation > 0.7, set saturation to 0.8-1.2; if saturation > 1.3, set color preservation to 0.3-0.6.
Overall Brightness _Brightness range

Fine-tunes the final brightness. 0.5 to 0.9 for darker, 1.0 for default, 1.1 to 1.5 for brighter.

Default: 1

Range: 0.5 to 1.5

Useful for fine-tuning the overall brightness to match the world's lighting.
